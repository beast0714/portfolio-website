02 / BIOMECHANICS · COMSOL

Femur load transfer

Used finite element modeling to inspect stress and displacement in a simplified femur model under an applied proximal load.

Read project notes

Mapped geometry, material assumptions and boundary conditions to the resulting stress field. A cross-section resultant and reaction balance would be stronger evidence of shaft load transfer than the color scale alone.

Can a second ankle sensor improve the comparison?

The gait study compares unilateral and bilateral sensing on the same recording sessions. The next step is a reproducible evaluation with held-out sessions and explicit limits on what simulated limping can tell us.

A follow-up to the COMSOL project will compare section forces and bending moments with support reactions, then test how the results change with mesh and boundary conditions.
